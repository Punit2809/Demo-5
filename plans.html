FAQ

Plans, Answered

What does the upfront fee cover?

It funds your complete career program — resume rewrite, coaching, marketing and dedicated placement support — for the full plan term.

How does the percentage-based fee work?

The percentage applies only to your first-year annual income, and only after you accept an offer. There are no hidden costs.

What is the 120-Day Placement Guarantee?

If you haven't received an offer within the guarantee window and meet the program requirements, our refund policy applies as written in your agreement.

Can I switch plans after enrolling?

Yes. If your goals change, you can upgrade at any time and only pay the difference between plans.

Do you support visa sponsorship?

We coordinate documentation and onboarding for eligible candidates, but do not arrange sponsorship or H-1B visas.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards, bank transfers and secure online payment — with transparent, written terms.
